HP / BETA / BURST - 0.5.2

HP AND BETA
The number is the permanent upgrade level, not a direct HP/energy amount.
Choose a higher target level. Each new level costs 3 cores.
If you do not have enough, enable Grant missing cores to supply and spend
only the missing upgrade credits. Leave it off to use collected cores only.
This does not mark fallen bodies or world pickups as collected.
MAX means the supported game limit: 6 upgrades in a normal game, 9 in NG+.
The editor cannot go beyond that cap. Higher limits require a game-data mod.
Equipment can also affect the numeric maximum displayed by the game.

BURST (THE RED ENERGY YOU IDENTIFIED)
Choose a Burst Expansion Gear variant.
Choose its destination:
- Inventory only: it is available in your Gear menu; equip it in game.
- Equip in gear slot: it replaces the gear in that unlocked slot immediately
  in the modified save. The old gear remains in your inventory.
The game calculates the capacity bonus after loading/equipping.
The same gear cannot be equipped twice. Other gear slots stay unchanged.
The editor does not unlock Burst skills or change the current bar fill.

WEAPON
WeaponCore quantities are editable in Consumables and items > Materials.
Direct weapon upgrade levels are not implemented. Spend the cores at a camp.

SAVING
Click Save copy in the main window. Current fields are collected and validated
automatically, and a permanent original backup is created.
Use the new executable from the 0.5.2 ZIP. Earlier GitHub releases may differ.
These new operations have structural/UI tests; running-game testing is pending.
