STELLAR BLADE - NG+ EQUIPMENT AND CORES - 0.7.0

1. Close the game and open your current main .sav in the editor.
2. Open NG+ equipment and cores. The header must say NG+ detected for Mk2.
3. Choose Chill NG+ preset, or select individual equipment and materials.
4. Review changes if desired, then Save copy from the main window.
5. Load the modified copy on your game PC and equip your preferred new items.

CHILL NG+ PRESET
89 Mk2 entries = 12 exospines at level 3 + 77 gear variants.
HP and Beta upgrade levels 9; weapon power level 40.
At least 999 unspent SP and 500,000 gold; existing higher balances are retained.
Core/module minimum stock 100: Weapon Core, Damaged Weapon Core, Drone module,
Tumbler expansion material, Omnibolt and Exospine expansion module.
Crafting/component minimum stock 1,000: three polymer/fiber materials,
three wafer types and three microcomponents.
Existing supported consumables get at least 99; existing ammo types at least 999.
No DLC currency or story-only Burst Core is granted by this preset.

UPGRADE COSTS AND STOCK ORDER
Minimum resource stock is granted first. Explicit item quantity edits come next.
Permanent upgrades then spend from the resulting inventory. HP/Beta use 3 matching
cores per level, and weapon power uses 1 Weapon Core per level. With Grant missing
upgrade credits, only an actual shortfall is granted and spent.
Example: weapon level 15 -> 40 with a 100-core stock grant leaves 75 cores.
An explicit manual stock of 30 takes precedence and leaves 5 after that upgrade.
Exospine levels are granted without module costs; no existing higher level is lost.

EQUIPMENT
The full supported list has 24 exospines and 154 gear entries: base and Mk2 variants.
All Mk2 equipment prepares 89 entries. All supported equipment prepares 178 in NG+
or the 89 base entries in a normal run. Choose a target exospine level from 1 to 3.
Gear saves use level 1; rarity and Mk2 are encoded in the item identifier.
New items stay in inventory. The preset does not automatically replace your loadout.
If a selected exospine is already equipped, its saved equipment copy receives the
same upgraded level while keeping the exact inventory identity. Other slots stay.
Duplicates, unknown identifiers, unsupported levels and missing genuine templates
are rejected. Obtain one real item from that category in game if a template is absent.

WHAT THE PRESET DOES NOT COMPLETE
Chests, world pickups, quests, story abilities and learned skills stay unchanged.
Source chests and bodies remain collectible; no pickup-blocking flag is applied.
Their exact item-to-world links could not be verified for this implementation.
Spend SP on your desired skills in game. Difficulty is not edited. The run remains
the same NG+ run; base-game saves are not converted into NG+ by this feature.
The official weapon cap is 15 normal / 40 NG+. HP/Beta caps remain 6 normal / 9 NG+.

BACKUPS AND VALIDATION
Save copy creates a permanent full original backup in CopiasSeguridad beside the
destination. Original files and existing copies are preserved.
The new operations have structural and UI tests, including GVAS/EVAS integrity,
but have not been tested inside the game on this PC. Verify loading, equipment,
upgrades, core balances, saving and reloading on your game PC.
