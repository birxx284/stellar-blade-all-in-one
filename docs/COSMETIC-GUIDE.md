# Cosmetic acquisition guide — 0.6.0

304 verified catalog entries. Descriptions explain the original acquisition route; granting an item does not complete its source chest, quest or shop purchase. DLC ownership is still required. Guide descriptions are concise English summaries, not full puzzle walkthroughs. Future game additions and arbitrary community mods are outside this verified list.

## Angel and special appearances

**Angel / Fusion — Symbol of Legacy:** earn the earrings through the Making New Memories ending. Equip them and activate an already-unlocked Tachy Mode. In the editor, choose Angel / Fusion guide → Prepare required item, then save a copy and equip the accessory in game. This does not finish the ending or create a permanent Nano Suit. [Official effect description](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/11/11/stellar-blades-nier-automata-dlc-photo-mode-launch-nov-20/).

**Permanent Angel — external mod:** [Playable Angel Outfit by Kaineriqa](https://www.nexusmods.com/stellarblade/mods/296) replaces Red Passion (BS_38). The author instructs placing its three mod files in SB/Content/Paks/~mods. Follow the author's current instructions and check compatibility. The editor can prepare Red Passion only. The mod is not bundled or installed; compatibility has not been tested here.

**Tachy transformation:** unlock the ability through the main story, then activate it in game. It is not a separate Nano Suit and the editor does not force its story unlock.

Unknown appearance-like IDs found in your loaded save are shown read-only in Uncatalogued owned. Their names and acquisition methods have not been verified.
